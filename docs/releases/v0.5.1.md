# PhoneMic 0.5.1

Soundboard clips now come through clean, the mic and the soundboard mute
separately, and both apps have a new look.

**Update the PC app and the phone app together.** A 0.5.0 PC app drops the
phone's soundboard audio from 0.5.1 (it doesn't know the new frame flag).

## Downloads

| | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) | `PhoneMic-windows-x64.zip`: unzip and run `PhoneMic.exe` |

## What's new

- **Clear soundboard audio.** Sounds were muffled: the phone encoded them as
  speech, and the PC's Voice focus treated music as noise. While a sound plays
  the phone now sends full-band audio at 96 kbps, and the PC skips noise
  removal for it. Your voice alone still uses the speech settings.
- **Two mutes.** **Mic** mutes only your voice; **Mute sounds** on the
  Soundboard screen mutes only the clips.
- **New look**, styled like studio hardware on both apps: graphite faceplates,
  LED lamps and keys, monospace readouts, and a round level meter. On the phone
  the meter ring is the Start/Stop key; on the PC clicking its centre mutes. A
  tally light shows ON AIR, MUTED or STANDBY.
  Themes are Black, Graphite and Silver; accents Signal (new default), Teal,
  Violet, Rose and Ice. Your saved theme and accent carry over by position.
